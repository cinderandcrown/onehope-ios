# Pastor walkthrough

A Sunday-morning check that a pastor can run the Staff Command Center without a developer. Use the sample pastor on a demo or preview host. Nothing on this path texts a family, charges a card, or sends a push.

Sign in with **Preview as sample pastor**. You land on Overview.

## 1. Sunday desk

Look at the cards. Each **Open** button goes to the screen that finishes that job.

**This week on Home** is the member app for the coming days. If a card, gathering, or message looks wrong, fix it in the steps below and come back.

**Latest message** says “No message is featured yet” until you feature one.

## 2. Home cards

Open **Communications**.

- Tap **Post “Sunday at 10:30”** to put a card on Home in one tap.
- Or write your own. Leave the times blank to show it now. Fill **Show starting** and **Hide after** when it should only run for a window. Those times are Central.
- **Preview** is what people see.
- **Archive** takes a card off Home. **Put back on Home** brings it back.

The built-in Sunday and Kids cards are not written back over a card you edited.

## 3. Events and serve days

Open **Events**.

- **Post Sunday gathering** or **Post Prayer night** uses the next real date. It does not silently pick a day a week out.
- A custom event needs a name, a place, a start, and an end. Capacity is optional.
- **Cancel event** takes it off the app. **Put back on the calendar** restores it.
- A serve day works the same way. Leave it empty until you have a real date.

## 4. Care

Open **Care**. If it asks, enter the 6-digit authenticator code and tap **Confirm it is me**.

- On a next step, choose a status, assign a staff person, set a due date, and write a staff note. **Save follow-up**. Nothing is texted or emailed.
- **Prayers to share**: **Share with the church** or **Keep private**.
- **Prayers someone reported**: the request is already off the wall. Share it again or leave it off.
- **Testimonies to approve**: share only when they already said yes.

## 5. Kids check-in

Open **Check-in**. This screen does not text families.

- Tap **Clock in** if you are on the kids team (kids director, kids team leader, or kids volunteer). Pickup stays closed until a second adult from the kids team clocks in. A pastor, finance teammate, or care teammate cannot be that second adult.
- **Volunteers clock in from the One Hope app, not this web desk.** Kids volunteers and team leaders do not have a staff sign-in, so they sign in to the app, open **More → Children's Church** (or the kids section on **Connect**), and tap **Clock in** (and **Clock out** when they leave). Staff on the kids team can use either place. Taking someone off the kids team also clocks them out.
- Filter by room. Tap **Check in** on a child. Read the 4-digit code to the adult, or **Copy code** / **Print label**.
- Pickup: choose an allowed adult and type the code. A person marked “Cannot pick up” is not in the list.
- Allergies stay off this screen.

## 6. People and access

Open **People**. Add a name and email. The code appears on the screen with **Copy code** and an expiry. Nothing is emailed.

On a phone, open **/claim**, type the email, the code, and a password twice.

**Undo add** works for 10 minutes, only for the person who added them, and only until they set a password. It is not shown on staff or Master Admin seats.

The role menu only lists roles you are allowed to give. Master Admin is not in that menu.

The first time a staff person signs in, leave the authenticator code blank. The setup screen shows a QR code and a setup key. After they enter the 6-digit code, the desk opens.

### Looking after people (Phase 2a)

**People** opens with **Everyone**: search by name, email or phone, and filter by stage, tag, or **Only quiet** (nobody has called, texted, visited, emailed or prayed with them in 30 days). Tap a name to open their page.

On a person's page:

- **Log a touch**: pick what you did (Called, Texted, Visited, Emailed, Prayed with, Note), how it went, and an optional note. It goes on their **Timeline** with your name and the time. Notes are encrypted and only staff who can open People see them. A private Note does not count as contact.
- If someone **asked us not to contact them**, pick that outcome. The page then shows a banner and blocks calls, texts, emails and visits. Praying and private notes still work. A pastor or care team member can tap **They said it is okay again**.
- **Stage** is where they are on the path: Guest, Coming back, Connected, Member, Serving, Leading.
- **Tags** group people. A **team** tag also names its leaders.

Who sees whom:

- Master Admin, pastors and the care team see everyone and manage tags.
- Kids directors see kids households and the kids team.
- Team leaders see people on teams they lead and people they added. They can only put people on their own teams.
- Finance, volunteers and members do not open People.

Nothing on People sends a message. Salvation and first-visit playbooks are not part of this slice; they will arrive switched off until a pastor turns them on.

## 7. Serving

Open **Serving**. Choose a person and a spot such as Door or Kids. Tap **Ask them to serve**. **Undo** works while it still says they are waiting. Nothing is texted.

## 8. This Sunday’s message

Open **Media**. If YouTube loaded, pick the message, optionally type a speaker and a passage, and tap **Feature on Home**. Overview **Latest message** shows that title. **Take off Home** clears it.

If YouTube did not load, wait and open the page again. Nothing else changes.

## 9. Push

Open **Push**. The screen says nothing was sent, because Apple push is not turned on. **Save draft** keeps the words. Phones do not buzz.

The ready-made notes are Sunday at 10:30, Kids check-in is open, and Prayer night. **Everyone with the app** is every person who has the app, guests included, not only people whose role is Member. Volunteers and Parents stay their own groups. The menu shows the count. Sends stay off until Apple push is actually connected.

When Apple push is actually connected, the button asks you to confirm the group and the count before anything goes out.

## 10. Security and the audit log

Open **Security**. Create a code for someone below your level who has not set a password yet, or save a role. Codes and role changes only reach roles below your own: a care teammate cannot seat finance or another care teammate, and a pastor cannot seat another pastor. A team leader can still add people, make those codes, and edit Home cards. A team leader cannot make a code for a pastor or a Master Admin, or give someone a role above their own. Only a Master Admin can make a code for a pastor or Master Admin seat.

Open **Admin**. Pick a from date and a to date, tap **Show**, then **Download spreadsheet**. The file is the same audit log, with times in Central. It does not contain passwords or codes.

## Stop

Sign out. Do not turn on live giving, Apple push, or texting from this walkthrough.
